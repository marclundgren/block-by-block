Questions

Who is this for?

Kids, teens, and adult beginners. Parents can set it up; kids can follow along.

Does my kid need to pay for an AI service?

No. The AI chapter runs a small model locally, so there is no account and no credit card.

What computer do we need?

A Windows PC or a Mac works great. A Raspberry Pi works too, just a little slower.
